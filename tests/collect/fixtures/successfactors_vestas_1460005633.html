Senior Process Engineer

The Process Engineer owns process and equipment performance in an assigned production area, leading PFMEA, change control, and front-line troubleshooting of automated equipment to meet SQDCP objectives. The role drives continuous improvement and supports new product and technology introduction in partnership with production, Lean Engineering, and headquarters. 

 

What you will do as a Senior Process Engineer

  • Take ownership of an assigned production area, working closely with production staff to meet the production plan. 

  • Lead Process Failure Mode and Effects Analysis (PFMEA) activities after Vestas Way to Market (VWTM) Gate 7. 

  • Ensure achievement of Safety, Quality, Delivery, Cost, and People (SQDCP) objectives and adherence to layout design, PFMEA requirements, Control Plans, Process Work Instructions (PWI), testing and tooling Requests for Proposals (RFPs), workforce balancing, Takt time planning, process parameters, and process capability.
