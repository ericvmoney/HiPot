Production Supervisor
Canonsburg, PA
Apply
Description

The Production Supervisor leads and develops a team of production union employees to safely manufacture power and distribution transformers that meet quality, delivery, and cost targets. This role plans and directs daily production activities, enforces safety and quality standards, and fosters a respectful, engaged, and accountable work environment. 


Requirements

  

• Plan, schedule, and assign daily work to meet production schedules, customer commitments, and on-time delivery goals across the transformer build process, including core cutting and stacking, coil winding, core-and-coil assembly, drying, tanking, and final assembly and test.

• Promote and enforce a strong safety culture; ensure compliance with OSHA regulations, lockout/tagout, confined space, hot work, and overhead crane and rigging requirements, and company safety policies, conduct safety meetings, and investigate incidents and near misses.

• Monitor production processes and output to ensure product quality, enforce build standards for winding, insulation, clearances, and cleanliness, and work with Quality, Engineering, and the Test department to resolve nonconformances and drive corrective actions.

• Supervise, coach, train, and evaluate production employees; conduct performance reviews and address performance and attendance issues in line with company policy.

• Administer and apply the collective bargaining agreement, where applicable, including handling grievances and working with Human Resources and Labor Relations.

• Ensure accurate recording of production data, labor hours, timekeeping, and material usage in the ERP/MES system.

• Coordinate with Materials, Maintenance, Engineering, and Shipping to minimize downtime and resolve bottlenecks.

• Drive continuous improvement initiatives (Lean, 5S, waste reduction) to improve efficiency, throughput, and cost.

• Participate in hiring, onboarding, and cross-training of team members.

• Maintain a clean, organized, and safe work area and ensure equipment is properly maintained.

Perform other duties as assigned


Qualifications:  

High school diploma or equivalent

• 4+ years of experience in a manufacturing or industrial production environment, including 1+ year in a lead or supervisory capacity.

• Working knowledge of OSHA safety requirements and safe work practices.

• Strong leadership, communication, and problem-solving skills.

• Proficiency with Microsoft Office and production/ERP systems.

• Ability to read and interpret work orders, build travelers, engineering drawings, and procedures.

Preferred

• Associate's or bachelor's degree in engineering, operations management, or a related field.

• Experience in transformer manufacturing (core, coil winding, assembly, or test) or in similar electrical equipment or heavy fabrication.

• Experience supervising in a unionized environment.

• Lean/Six Sigma training or certification.

• Overhead crane operation or rigging experience.

Knowledge, Skills, and Abilities

• Ability to motivate and manage a diverse workforce.

• Sound decision-making in a fast-paced environment.

• Strong organizational and time-management skills.

• Working knowledge of quality systems (e.g., ISO 9001).

• Ability to handle conflict fairly and professionally.

Physical Requirements and Work Environment

• Work is performed in a manufacturing plant environment with exposure to noise, heat or cold, insulating materials and transformer oil, and moving equipment and overhead cranes.

• Required to wear appropriate PPE, including safety glasses, hearing protection, and steel-toed footwear.

• Frequent standing and walking; occasionally lift up to 50 lbs.

• May be required to work overtime, weekends, or alternate shifts based on production needs.
