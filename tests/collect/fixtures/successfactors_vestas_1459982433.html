Vestas is the energy industry's global partner in sustainable energy solutions. We design, manufacture, install, and service wind turbines. Since 2010, the Nacelles Factory in Brighton has proudly manufactured nacelles (engines) and hubs for wind turbines within 265,000 square feet of production space. 

The Logistics Supervisor is responsible to oversee, and coordinate activities of workers concerned with shipping and movement of turbine blades, storing, inventorying, ordering, receiving, issuing materials, supplies, tools, equipment, and parts, in stockroom, warehouse, or yard.  

  

 What does a Warehouse Supervisor do?  

  • Plan and direct shipping and warehouse operations in alignment with the production plan, ensuring adherence to quality assurance standards and best practices for inventory accuracy, stock levels, WIP, and inventory days.
