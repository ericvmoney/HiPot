Company Overview

 

Imagine a piloted air taxi that takes off vertically, then quietly carries you and your fellow passengers over the congested city streets below, enabling you to spend more time with the people and places that matter most. At Joby, we've been working to make that dream a reality since 2009 and we're now in the final stages of certifying our aircraft with the FAA. With plans to launch our aircraft in the US and Dubai, we're now scaling manufacturing and preparing for the launch of our commercial service.

Overview

Joby Aviation is looking for talented mechanics, technicians, and fabricators to join our Final Integration and Assembly team. In this role, you will be trained to assemble all the parts, components, harnesses, avionics, interiors, landing gear, and systems into the final aircraft needed to prepare it for flight. If you are hardworking and excited about participating in the development of next–generation, innovative aircraft, and if you take pride in doing high-quality work, then we want to talk to you. On our team, you will contribute to the manufacture and assembly of an innovative aircraft that will change the future of flying.

 

We are a tight-knit team that likes to work hard, and we enjoy what we do. We have multiple openings for several roles at unique various experience levels. Let us know how your passion and experience can help us build the future of air transportation!

Responsibilities

  • Perform final mechanical, electrical, and structural integration and assembly of aircraft systems, including avionics, harnesses, interiors, and landing gear

  • Read, interpret, and execute build plans using complex engineering drawings, schematics, and work instructions

  • Conduct pre-inspections of integrated assemblies to verify structural and system integrity prior to submitting work for final quality assurance inspection

  • Perform system testing, basic troubleshooting, and verification of mechanical, hydraulic, and electrical components

  • Collaborate closely with engineering and quality teams to support the transition of components from research & development to flight-worthy production parts

  • Maintain a clean, organized, and safe workspace while documenting assembly steps and quality metrics in accordance with company manufacturing standards

Required

  • High School Diploma, GED, or equivalent relevant work experience

  • At least 2+ years of hands-on experience in aviation, aerospace, automotive, or R&D mechanical systems, including a solid general knowledge of vehicle assembly, maintenance, and repair

  • Ability to read and interpret engineering drawings, blueprints, schematics, and work instructions

  • Familiarity with standard aircraft hardware, fasteners, torque values, and safety-wiring techniques

  • Experience working within robust quality assurance processes, with a demonstrated ability to identify high-quality work and pre-inspect assembly steps

  • Familiarity with and ability to identify all key components needed for complete mechanical, hydraulic, and electrical assemblies

  • Understanding of the different requirements for producing both flight-worthy production parts and research & development parts, respectively

  • Proven ability to work independently and multi-task efficiently in a fast-paced environment

  • Advanced understanding of production processes, test equipment, and hand/power tools

Desired

  • Associate's or higher technical degree
  • Ability to act as a mentor, trainer, or advisor to other technicians utilizing gained expertise (Highly desired for S3-level applicants)
  • FAA Airframe and Powerplant (A&P) rating
  • FAA Inspection Authorization (IA) rating
  • Pilots license     

 

Compensation at Joby is a combination of base pay and Restricted Stock Units (RSUs). The target base pay for this position is $25-$39/hour. The compensation package will be determined by job-related knowledge, skills, and experience.

Joby also offers a comprehensive benefits package, including paid time off, healthcare benefits, a 401(k) plan with a company match, an employee stock purchase plan (ESPP), short-term and long-term disability coverage, life insurance, and more.
