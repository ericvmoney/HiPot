### Gaps this board can own

1. **Discipline, climate subsector and startup stage together.** Hardware FYI has disciplines but treats energy as one industry of ten. Climate boards have subsectors but stop at "electrical / mechanical / hardware". Nobody has all three.
2. **The disciplines hardware people search by.** Controls, power electronics, battery and cell, test, and systems are missing from every climate board. Controls is missing even from Hardware FYI.
3. **The investor boards hold the right companies and are barely searchable.** Breakthrough, EIP and The Engine list over 4,000 roles between them behind a keyword box. No one has normalized them under a hardware taxonomy.
4. **Resume-based matching for hardware.** Built In's is generic. Hardware FYI's beta takes a text brief. No climate board offers candidate-side resume parsing, and none does it on the candidate's device.
5. **Open search.** Wellfound, YC and Otta gate search behind accounts. climate.jobs and Terra.do charge candidates. Both are things this board has ruled out.

Hardware FYI is the closest competitor and the one to study before M1.

### Data sources

- Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby each publish a no-login job feed per company. These are what the old hub reads. The agent did not read the vendors' general terms or any robots files; that review is still owed.
- Investor boards (run on Getro or Consider) are good for discovering companies and finding which feed each one uses. Pull the roles from the company's own feed, not from the investor board.
- ClimateTechList sells licences to its job data, so treat it as a place to find company names by hand, not something to copy in bulk.

## 2. Name candidates

How each was checked: a Bing search in the browser for the name alongside jobs, company and startup terms; a DNS lookup of the .com, .jobs, .co, .io and .work addresses; and a visit to the .com where one answered. **Not done: a trademark search, or a registrar check.** "No DNS record" suggests an address is unregistered but does not prove it.

### Shortlist

| # | Name | Why it works | What the check found |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **HiPot** | A hipot test is the high-potential test every high-voltage product must pass. Reads as "high potential" to everyone else. Short, and instantly recognized by exactly the audience | No job board, recruiter or startup using it. hipot.com belongs to SCI, a maker of hipot testers. hipot.co is for sale. hipot.jobs and hipot.io have no DNS record |
| 2 | **Spinning Reserve** | Grid term for capacity that is already online and ready to pick up load. That is a talent pool, said in the audience's own language | No brand, podcast or company found. spinningreserve.com is parked for sale at HugeDomains. .jobs, .co and .io have no DNS record |
| 3 | **Work Function** | Physics term for the energy needed to free an electron. Also reads literally as "job function", which is the board's main filter | No company found. workfunction.com is registered with no live site. .jobs, .co and .io have no DNS record. WorkFusion, an automation software company, sounds similar |
| 4 | **Charge Carriers** | The particles that carry current, and a pun on careers | No brand found. chargecarriers.com has no DNS record. chargecarrier.com (singular) is registered and did not respond |
| 5 | **Busbar** | The conductor everything connects to. Six letters | busbar.com is registered with no live site. Small existing uses: a Voltplan tool called "The Busbar" and an electrical-code calculator app. .jobs, .co and .io have no DNS record |
| 6 | **Golden Unit** | The known-good reference unit on a test line | No brand found. goldenunit.com is for sale at $4,995 |
| 7 | **Potential Difference** | Voltage, and "making a difference". The most direct climate reading of the set | No brand found. potentialdifference.com is for sale at $7,595. Long to type |
| 8 | **Magic Smoke** | The hardware in-joke: every chip runs on magic smoke until you let it out. Gets attention; ironic on a clean tech site | No company found. magicsmoke.com is parked |

**Recommendation: HiPot**, with **Spinning Reserve** as the alternative if you want something that says energy rather than hardware. HiPot is the only one that is short, means something specific to a hardware engineer, and carries a second meaning that works for people who have never held a test probe.

**Nameplate** is taken as a site name (below) but would suit the persona file: a nameplate is the plate on a machine that states its ratings. "Download your nameplate."
