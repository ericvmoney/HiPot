How these ranges are built

Posted is what the companies on this board advertise right now: the median low end to the median high end of the pay they published, with the number of postings behind it. The smaller figure is the median midpoint. A dash means fewer than three postings, too few to show a figure.

Only published pay counts. A role enters only when its posting states a range in US dollars. Nothing is estimated, and a missing range is never filled in. Yearly salaries and hourly pay are kept apart and never converted into each other.

Role is the first engineering or science discipline for engineers and scientists, and the role type for everyone else: technicians, trades, supply chain and so on. Level comes from the job title; "Level not stated" collects titles that give none.

Market is a metro area as the Census Bureau draws it. A role listed in two metro areas counts in both. Remote (US) collects remote roles, and All US counts every role in the United States once.

Government wage data (what people in a matching occupation are actually paid, from the Bureau of Labor Statistics) is planned and not yet included.
