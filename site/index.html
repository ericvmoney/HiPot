    How this board is built

    Every role comes from the company's own job board. The board reads each company's public job feed and links each role to the posting there. Nothing comes from an aggregator or a search result. A role that leaves the company's board leaves this one at the next refresh.

    Companies are screened before their roles appear, for climate fit under a written rubric and for size: a startup here has fewer than 1,000 people.

    The company screen is provisional. The climate tier and reason shown for each company are a first pass. They have not yet been checked against the company's own site or approved.

    Core
    The company's main product directly cuts emissions or electrifies something that burns fuel.
    Enabling
    Electric by design and likely to displace a dirtier alternative, though cutting emissions is not its purpose.

    Pay is shown only when the employer published it. It is never estimated. Role type, level and discipline are assigned automatically from the title and posting, so read the posting before judging fit.

    Searching needs no login. This page sets no cookies and does no tracking; its fonts load from Google Fonts.

    The name comes from the hipot test, which puts a high potential across a product's insulation to prove it holds before the product ships.
