    How this page is built

    Every listed company is here, clean tech and the categories next to it, including companies with no open roles. Open roles, new roles and locations come from the board: a company's locations are where its open roles are, and with none, its headquarters.

    The pay note sums up the pay its open roles publish, using the board's market comparison: each role's midpoint against comparable published ranges (same role group and level, in its metro area, or across the US). Above or below market means the median of its graded roles is more than 10 percent from the market; at market, within it. Too few to say means fewer than 3 roles could be compared; unlisted salaries means none of its open roles publishes pay. Nothing is estimated.

    How we read its jobs: read daily from its job board; read weekly where the job board's robots.txt asks for less; or not read automatically, when we have no job board for it that we can read. Those companies may well be hiring: follow their careers link.
