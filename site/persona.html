Build your persona

Turn your resume into a short persona.yml: the kinds of work, disciplines, level, skills and preferences HiPot can use to find roles for you. The page proposes; you check every field and save the file. Later it will rank roles for you.

Your resume stays on this page. It is read by code running in your browser and is never sent anywhere: the page makes no network request that carries your resume, and it stores nothing in your browser. Your name, email, phone number, street address and employer names are removed before anything is kept, and none of them is written to the file. Close the tab and it is gone.

Step 1

Read your resume

The text you see here is what the rules read. Check it, especially from a two-column PDF, where lines can come out in the wrong order. A scanned PDF has no text to read.
